# 📖 Guidance of the Rightly Guided  
**Lives and Leadership of the Four Khulafāʾ al-Rāshidīn**

---

## 📚 Book Overview
This book is a detailed, comprehensive exploration of the lives and leadership of the **Four Rightly Guided Caliphs** (*Khulafāʾ al-Rāshidīn*). Spanning over **125,000 words**, it delves deeply into the era of:

- **Abu Bakr al-Ṣiddīq (RA)** – First Khalīfah  
- **ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb (RA)** – Second Khalīfah  
- **ʿUthmān ibn ʿAffān (RA)** – Third Khalīfah  
- **ʿAlī ibn Abī Ṭālib (RA)** – Fourth Khalīfah  

Each chapter contains multiple detailed parts offering historical depth, moral lessons, and references — providing a holistic understanding of Islamic leadership during a pivotal era.

---

## 📖 Book Description
*"Guidance of the Rightly Guided"* is more than a historical account — it is a moral and leadership guide.  
The book examines the political, social, and spiritual dimensions of early Islam, highlighting how each Khalīfah faced unique challenges and made distinctive contributions to governance, justice, and unity.  

Key highlights include:
- Historical narrative with deep analysis  
- Leadership lessons grounded in Qur’an and Sunnah  
- Detailed accounts of challenges and reforms  
- References for further study  

This work is a must-read for historians, scholars, students of Islamic leadership, and anyone seeking timeless wisdom from the golden era of Islam.
